Policy-Based Trajectory Clustering

NeurIPS 2025 Submission by Xinqi Wang et al.

🌌 What is Policy Clustering and Why Does It Matter?

Policy clustering is a technique in offline reinforcement learning that groups trajectories based on the policies that generated them. Different policies can produce vastly different behaviors, and separating them can improve learning efficiency and interpretability.

  • Reduces interference from suboptimal policies, improving stability.
  • Enhances interpretability of policy behaviors for debugging and refinement.
  • Supports semi-supervised fine-tuning on limited labeled trajectories.

🔹 Two Methods Overview

PG-Kmeans

An EM-style clustering approach that alternates between training behavior cloning policies and assigning trajectories based on action likelihoods.

CAAE

Inspired by VQ-VAE, this method learns latent representations of trajectories and attracts them to Gaussian centers for clustering.
